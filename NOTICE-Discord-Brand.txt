WHAT WAS DONE TO IT: downscaled to 33x25, 66x50 and 99x75 with Lanczos, which
are exact reductions of the 528x400 source (528:400 = 33:25), so no scale
distorts the mark. The transparent background, the geometry and the official
blurple are unmodified. The app renders it with `.renderingMode(.original)`, so
it is never recoloured.

WHY IT IS NOT A FONT GLYPH. This row first drew `fa-discord` from the bundled
Nerd Fonts subset. The LICENCE side of that was clean - Font Awesome Free ships
under CC BY 4.0 and the attribution is in App/Fonts/NerdFonts-LICENSE.txt - but
CC BY 4.0 grants no TRADEMARK rights (section 2(b)(2)), and Discord's brand
policy asks for the official mark in an approved colour rather than a
third-party redraw. The owner chose the official asset on 2026-09-17.
